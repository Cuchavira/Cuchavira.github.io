Appendix A: Researcher Field Notes Template

Project: Touch-to-Meaning Tactile Learning Project
Activity: One-Day Timeline: Day/Night and Morning/Noon/Afternoon
Date: June 16, 2026
Purpose: Take structured observer field notes during the classroom activity and connect notes with videos, photos, student work IDs, and teacher follow-up after class.

Note-Taking Principles

  • Record observable facts before adding interpretations or follow-up questions.

  • Use the June 16 flexible stage labels as anchors, not strict task boundaries.

  • Do not aim for full transcription. Prioritize key moments, time ranges, evidence sources, and questions for teacher follow-up.

  • Teachers do not need to write field notes. Observers write the notes; teachers may review or clarify interpretation after class.

  • Only student work receives work IDs. Do not assign IDs to loose materials.
